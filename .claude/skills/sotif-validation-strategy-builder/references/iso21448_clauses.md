# ISO 21448:2022 Validation Clauses

## Section 10 — SOTIF Validation Specification

Defines the plan for proving the system achieves its SOTIF goals.

## Section 11 — SOTIF Validation Implementation

Describes the execution, monitoring, and tracking of validation evidence against acceptance criterion.

## Key Concepts

- Validation evidence comes from real-world driving, simulation, proving ground, and field monitoring
- Acceptance criterion must be quantified and justified
- Residual risk of Unknown Unsafe scenarios must be demonstrably below acceptable threshold
- Post-deployment field monitoring continues to track residual risk trends
