# Triggering Conditions Methodology

Triggering conditions are factors that activate or expose functional insufficiencies. They are the bridge between specification and real-world scenarios.

## Categories

- **Environmental**: Weather, lighting, road condition, visibility
- **Sensor**: Contamination, occlusion, calibration drift, EMI
- **Road Infrastructure**: Lane markings, geometry, construction, signage
- **Road User**: Pedestrian, cyclist, motorcycle, vehicle, animal behavior
- **System State**: Warm-up, OTA update, battery low, degraded mode
- **Driver State**: Inattention, drowsiness, hands-off, mode confusion
- **Combined**: Two or more simultaneous triggering conditions (Cartesian)

Each condition is assigned a likelihood (very rare, rare, occasional, common, frequent).

## Usage

Triggering conditions map directly to scenario test design, field monitoring filters, and validation coverage planning.
