# ISO 21448:2022 Key Clauses

## Section 5 — Safety Assessment

ISO 21448 defines four scenario quadrants:

1. **Known Safe** — function operates as specified; outcome is safe
2. **Known Unsafe** — function fails; outcome is hazardous; identified and mitigated via design
3. **Unknown Safe** — scenario not in specification; outcome happens to be safe
4. **Unknown Unsafe** — scenario not in specification; outcome is hazardous (residual risk target)

## Section 6 — SOTIF Concept

Documents the Operational Design Domain (ODD) and expected performance envelope.

## Section 7 — SOTIF Specification

Technical specification of the intended functionality performance limits.

## Section 8 — SOTIF Implementation

Design and realization; mitigation of known insufficiencies.

## Section 10 & 11 — Validation & Verification

Validation strategy to demonstrate residual risk of Unknown Unsafe scenarios is acceptable.
