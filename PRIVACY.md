# Privacy Policy for Puff the Candles

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Puff the Candles is a birthday-candle game for young children. It is made by an independent developer (GitHub user gurubhingarde). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

## Microphone

The app asks for permission to use the microphone so a child can blow on the phone to put the candles out.

- Sound from the microphone is analysed on the phone, in real time, only to measure whether someone is blowing.
- Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. It is discarded immediately after each moment is measured.
- The microphone is used only while the game is open on screen, and it switches off when the app goes into the background.
- A grown-up can turn the microphone off with the "Mic" button at the top of the game, or deny the permission in Android settings. The game still works by tapping the candles.

## Other information

- The app saves one setting on the phone: the number of candles last chosen. This never leaves the device.
- The app does not collect names, contact details, location, photos, device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), or usage data.
- The app does not contain third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- The app does not load content from the internet or send any data to any server.

## Children

The app is designed for children under 5 and follows the Google Play Families Policies. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the contact email shown on the app's Google Play Store page.
