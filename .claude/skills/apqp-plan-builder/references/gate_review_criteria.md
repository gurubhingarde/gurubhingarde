# APQP Phase Gate Review Criteria Reference

This document defines the gate review structure and exit criteria for each of the five APQP phases. Gate reviews are formal checkpoints where leadership assesses phase completion and authorizes progression to the next phase.

## Gate Review Overview

**Purpose:** Gate reviews ensure that:
- All phase deliverables are complete and quality-assured
- Cross-functional team alignment exists on schedule and risks
- Customer expectations are met before transitioning to the next phase
- Risks are identified and mitigation plans in place
- Budget and schedule remain on track

**Attendees (typical):**
- Program Manager (facilitator/owner)
- Quality Engineer or Quality Lead
- Design Engineer or Design Lead
- Manufacturing Engineer or Plant Manager
- Supplier Quality (if suppliers involved)
- Customer representative (optional but recommended for Gates 1–3)
- Finance/Business (as needed)

**Meeting Agenda (typical):**
1. Review and sign-off of phase deliverables
2. Discussion of open issues, risks, lessons learned
3. Customer feedback (if customer present)
4. Schedule and budget status
5. Decision: Proceed, Hold, or Rework
6. Action items for next phase

**Decision outcomes:**
- **Proceed:** All exit criteria met; authorization to move to next phase
- **Hold:** Criteria partially met; conditions or follow-up actions required before progression
- **Rework:** Significant gaps; phase work must be revised before re-review

## Gate 1: Plan and Define Program Review

**Phase:** Phase 1 (Plan and Define Program)

**Timing:** Typically 4–8 weeks after program kickoff

**Key Deliverables to Review:**
- Voice of Customer (VOC) summary
- Customer Requirements Review (CRR)
- Preliminary BOM and process flow
- Design goals and targets
- Reliability/durability targets
- Preliminary SC/CC list
- Team charter and APQP schedule
- Business case and approval

**Gate 1 Exit Criteria (decision to proceed to Phase 2):**

1. **Voice of Customer Complete**
   - Customer inputs documented (interviews, surveys, specifications)
   - All stated requirements captured
   - Ambiguities resolved with customer
   - CSL and CTQ requirements clearly defined

2. **Customer Requirements Review Approved**
   - Drawing, specification, test standards confirmed
   - Packaging, delivery, volume requirements defined
   - Warranty and reliability expectations documented
   - Special customer process requirements (e.g., PPAP level, testing) identified
   - Customer signature/approval obtained

3. **Preliminary BOM Adequate**
   - Key components identified
   - Make/buy decisions preliminary defined
   - Long-lead suppliers flagged
   - Cost estimate within business case target

4. **Preliminary Process Flow Feasible**
   - High-level process flow documented
   - Equipment and facility requirements estimated
   - Capacity at planned volume achievable
   - Make/assemble/test sequence logical

5. **Design and Reliability Targets Defined**
   - Performance targets quantified (dimensions, functions, durability)
   - Cost target agreed
   - Reliability targets (MTBF, warranty, design life) set
   - Environmental stress profile defined

6. **Preliminary SC/CC List Initiated**
   - Customer critical characteristics identified
   - Regulatory/safety critical characteristics noted
   - List to be finalized in Phase 2
   - Symbols on customer drawing understood

7. **Team Organized and Committed**
   - Core team members assigned and available
   - Roles and responsibilities clearly defined
   - Cross-functional participation confirmed (Design, Manufacturing, Quality, Supplier Quality, etc.)
   - Team kickoff meeting held and documented

8. **APQP Schedule Realistic**
   - Phase 2 start date confirmed
   - Key design review milestones scheduled
   - Gate 2 target date set (typically 4–6 months out)
   - Supplier deadlines clear
   - Resource availability confirmed

9. **Business Plan Approved**
   - Revenue and cost projections reasonable
   - Margin and profitability targets met
   - Investment (tooling, equipment, training) budgeted
   - Management sign-off obtained

10. **No Critical Blockers Remaining**
    - Customer contact and responsiveness confirmed
    - Supplier long-lead items ordered if required
    - Facility capacity not a constraint
    - No regulatory or technical show-stoppers identified

**Risk Assessment at Gate 1:**
- Identify program risks (schedule, technical, supply chain, customer)
- Document risk owners and mitigation plans
- Escalate critical risks to leadership

**Customer Involvement:**
- Customer quality/engineering representative present or remote
- Customer approval of CRR and SC/CC list obtained
- Customer questions and expectations addressed

## Gate 2: Design Confirmation Review

**Phase:** Phase 2 (Product Design and Development)

**Timing:** Typically 5–7 months after Gate 1

**Key Deliverables to Review:**
- Design FMEA (DFMEA) summary
- Design for Assembly/Manufacturability (DFA/DFM) report
- Design Verification Plan (DVP) and preliminary Report (DVR)
- Design Review reports (DR1, DR2, DR3)
- Prototype test results
- Final (or nearly final) engineering drawings and specifications
- Finalized SC/CC list with traceability to drawings
- Supplier APQP cascade acknowledgment

**Gate 2 Exit Criteria (decision to proceed to Phase 3):**

1. **Design FMEA Complete and Risk-Mitigated**
   - All significant failure modes identified and analyzed
   - High-RPN items (> threshold, e.g., RPN > 200) mitigated or accepted
   - Design changes implemented or documented for risk reduction
   - Mitigation effectiveness verified
   - DFMEA sign-off by Design Lead and Quality Lead

2. **Design Verification Plan Comprehensive**
   - All customer requirements mapped to verification tests
   - Test protocols defined (test method, acceptance criteria, sample size)
   - Prototype test strategy clear
   - Test equipment and resources available

3. **Design Verification Results Acceptable**
   - Prototype testing complete (or significantly complete)
   - All critical design requirements validated (dimensional, functional, environmental)
   - Performance meets or exceeds targets
   - Reliability testing underway or complete with acceptable results
   - No unresolved failures

4. **Design Reviews Conclusive**
   - DR1 (Preliminary Design Review) complete: concept approved
   - DR2 (Interim Design Review) complete: design progressing, no major issues
   - DR3 (Final Design Review) complete or planned: design mature, ready for process design
   - Prototype breadboard, prototype, or design validation hardware demonstrated
   - Design team confidence high

5. **Design for Assembly/Manufacturability Addressed**
   - Component count minimized
   - Assembly complexity acceptable
   - Manufacturing sequence logical
   - No identified design obstacles to production
   - Feedback from Manufacturing Engineer integrated

6. **Engineering Drawings and Specifications Near-Final**
   - All major subassemblies have drawings
   - Material specifications defined
   - Tolerances set (per ASME, ISO, customer standard)
   - Assembly drawings and BOM finalized
   - Electrical/software specifications documented (for complex products)
   - Minor changes expected in Phase 3, no major rework

7. **Special Characteristics List Finalized**
   - All SC and CC from customer drawing and DFMEA identified
   - Regulatory critical characteristics included
   - SC/CC traced to engineering drawings (symbol correlation)
   - Measurement method preliminary assigned
   - List stable, no expected additions

8. **Supplier APQP Cascade Complete**
   - All suppliers with make-to-print items have received APQP inputs
   - Drawings, specifications, PPAP level, schedule communicated
   - Supplier acknowledgment and APQP roadmap received
   - Supplier constraints and long-lead items identified

9. **No Critical Design Risks Remaining**
   - All unresolved design questions addressed
   - Design margins adequate for tolerance stack-up
   - Reliability predictions acceptable
   - Field failure risk low

10. **Schedule Realistic for Phase 3 and Beyond**
    - Phase 3 (process design) start date confirmed
    - PFMEA planning and process design timeline realistic
    - Gate 3 target date (trial run preparation) set
    - No anticipated delays to pilot run schedule

**Risk Assessment at Gate 2:**
- Identify design and manufacturing risks
- Update risk register with supplier feedback
- Adjust mitigation plans if new risks emerged

**Customer Involvement:**
- Customer quality/engineering review results
- Customer approval of design, SC/CC list, and PPAP level
- Prototype demonstration if possible (especially for complex products)

## Gate 3: Process Confirmation Review

**Phase:** Phase 3 (Process Design and Development)

**Timing:** Typically 3–4 months before planned Production Trial Run (Phase 4)

**Key Deliverables to Review:**
- Complete process flow chart and work instructions
- Process FMEA (PFMEA) summary and risk mitigation
- Characteristics Matrix (product-to-process traceability)
- Preliminary process capability study plan and sampling strategy
- MSA (Gage R&R) plan and measurement methods
- Pre-Launch Control Plan (PLCP) and reaction plans
- Packaging specifications
- Floor plan/facility layout

**Gate 3 Exit Criteria (decision to proceed to Phase 4 – Production Trial Run):**

1. **Process Design Documented**
   - Detailed process flow chart (each operation, equipment, cycle time, sequence)
   - Work instructions drafted or finalized for all operations
   - Machine setup procedures documented
   - Tool and fixture specifications defined
   - Safety and ergonomic considerations addressed

2. **Process FMEA Complete and Controls Defined**
   - All process failure modes identified
   - High-RPN items mitigated with process controls (fixtures, sensors, procedures)
   - Preventive measures (designed into process) vs. reactive measures (inspection) identified
   - PFMEA sign-off by Manufacturing Lead and Quality Lead

3. **Characteristics Matrix Complete**
   - All product SC/CC linked to specific process variables
   - Traceability clear: which operation(s) control each characteristic
   - Measurement point and method identified for each characteristic
   - Control method (100% inspect, AQL sampling, SPC charting) preliminary assigned

4. **Preliminary Process Capability Study Plan Approved**
   - Statistical sampling plan for trial run defined (sample size, frequency)
   - Cpk/Ppk targets and acceptance criteria set (e.g., ≥ 1.33 for trial run)
   - Confidence level and decision rules defined
   - SPC charting strategy (control limits calculation, in-control rules)
   - Reaction plan for out-of-control signals defined

5. **MSA Plan Finalized**
   - All gaging methods identified (CMM, fixtures, functional testers, environmental chambers)
   - Gage R&R study protocol defined (sample size, runs, operators)
   - Acceptance criteria set (e.g., < 10% for critical, < 30% for important)
   - Measurement budget (tolerance allocation) adequate for each characteristic
   - Equipment availability confirmed

6. **Process Capacity Confirmed at Planned Volume**
   - Cycle time analysis complete
   - Equipment throughput sufficient for planned volume
   - Bottleneck operations identified and mitigated
   - Shift/manning requirements defined
   - No anticipated capacity constraint to SOP rate

7. **Pre-Launch Control Plan Approved**
   - Non-conformance definition clear (what triggers reaction plan)
   - Escalation path defined (supervisor, quality, management, customer)
   - Containment procedure for out-of-spec parts (hold, rework, scrap, customer notification)
   - Root cause investigation process (RCCA approach)
   - Preventive action process to address systemic issues

8. **Packaging Specifications Complete**
   - Packaging materials and dimensions finalized
   - Barcode/label design approved
   - Customer logo and branding requirements met
   - Stacking and weight limits confirmed
   - Environmental protection (moisture, vibration) adequate

9. **Facility and Equipment Ready**
   - Equipment installation on schedule
   - Facility layout finalized (material flow, WIP staging, storage)
   - Utilities (power, water, compressed air) adequate
   - Safety systems in place
   - Environmental controls (temperature, humidity) adequate for process

10. **Operator Training Plan in Place**
    - Training curriculum defined
    - Trainer identified and qualified
    - Completion date before trial run confirmed
    - Competency assessment method defined

11. **Supplier Process Readiness Confirmed**
    - Supplier PFMEA received and reviewed
    - Supplier process capability plan aligned with main program
    - Supplier delivery schedule to support trial run confirmed
    - Supplier audit results acceptable (if conducted)

12. **No Critical Process Risks Remaining**
    - Process capability risk low (confidence in Cpk ≥ 1.33 achievable)
    - Supply chain delays not anticipated
    - Tooling and fixture availability confirmed
    - No unresolved process design questions

**Risk Assessment at Gate 3:**
- Update risk register with process and supply chain risks
- Identify trial run risks (equipment, staffing, material shortages)
- Plan contingencies (backup equipment, extra staffing)

**Customer Involvement:**
- Customer review of process flow and control plan (if required by customer)
- Customer approval of packaging and traceability approach (if applicable)
- Customer feedback on trial run plan timing and scope

## Gate 4: Production Readiness Review

**Phase:** Phase 4 (Product and Process Validation) – conducted after Production Trial Run (PRR)

**Timing:** Typically 2–4 weeks after trial run completion

**Key Deliverables to Review:**
- Production Trial Run report (setup, adjustments, yield, defects)
- Measurement System Analysis (Gage R&R) results
- Preliminary Process Capability Study (PPC) data and SPC charts
- Production Validation Testing report
- PPAP submission package (ready for customer)
- Production Control Plan (final)
- Sustained production @ rate (2–4 weeks evidence)
- Quality Planning Sign-Off documentation

**Gate 4 Exit Criteria (decision to proceed to SOP and Phase 5):**

1. **Production Trial Run Successful**
   - Trial run completed at planned volume and production rate
   - Setup and run-in time reasonable
   - Process adjustments minimal (no major rework required)
   - Yield acceptable (defect rate < 5% for non-critical processes, as appropriate)
   - No critical equipment failures or bottlenecks
   - Lessons learned documented

2. **Measurement System Analysis Adequate**
   - Gage R&R studies completed on all critical measuring equipment
   - Repeatability and reproducibility < acceptance threshold (e.g., < 10% for critical)
   - All gages accepted for production use
   - Measurement uncertainty accounted for in tolerance stack-up
   - Operator training on gaging complete

3. **Preliminary Process Capability Meets Targets**
   - SPC data collected on minimum 100 parts per characteristic (preferably 125+)
   - Cpk/Ppk calculated for all SC/CC
   - All critical characteristics meet minimum Cpk ≥ 1.33 (or customer requirement, e.g., 1.67)
   - Distribution approximately normal (check histograms)
   - Control limits set and plotted on control charts
   - No special cause variation detected (process in statistical control)

4. **Production Validation Testing Complete**
   - Dimensional inspection (CMM or go/no-go) on trial lot: 100% or AQL
   - Functional testing on trial lot: test results all pass
   - Environmental testing (if applicable): thermal, vibration, humidity — results acceptable
   - Material certification for all materials: certificates obtained
   - Reliability testing (if applicable): results acceptable or testing plan for continued monitoring

5. **PPAP Submission Package Ready**
   - Customer PPAP level determined (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 per AIAG PPAP manual)
   - First Article Inspection Report (FAIR) complete
   - Material and performance test data compiled
   - SPC charts, capability data ready
   - Supplier PPAP form signed (PSW)
   - PPAP submission ready for customer (all required documents per level)

6. **Production Control Plan Finalized**
   - Ongoing control strategy defined for all SC/CC (100% inspect, AQL sampling, SPC charting)
   - Sampling frequency and sample size for AQL-based inspection defined
   - SPC charting parameters (control limits, subgroup size) finalized
   - Reaction plan for out-of-control or non-conforming parts documented
   - Escalation path to customer (if applicable per agreement)
   - Frequency of control plan review (quarterly, annually) defined

7. **Sustained Production at Planned Rate Demonstrated**
   - Production run continued for minimum 2–4 weeks at production rates
   - SPC charts show stability over time
   - Defect rate consistent and low
   - Equipment uptime adequate (e.g., > 90%)
   - On-time delivery to schedule demonstrated
   - No unforeseen issues

8. **Quality Planning Sign-Off Obtained**
   - Customer quality/engineering sign-off on PPAP package
   - Supplier quality manager sign-off on process and control plan
   - Program manager sign-off on readiness for SOP
   - Finance/business sign-off on business metrics
   - All authorized signatures on Gate 4 review form

9. **PPAP Approval from Customer**
   - If PPAP level 3, 4, or 5: customer approval letter or notification received
   - If level 1 or 2: supplier retention of PPAP documentation confirmed
   - Approval conditions or exceptions noted and mitigation planned
   - PPAP follow-up items (if any) assigned and tracked

10. **No Critical Production Risks Remaining**
    - Process stable and repeatable
    - Supply chain tested (suppliers delivered on time and to quality)
    - Equipment proven reliable
    - Staffing and operator competency confirmed

**Risk Assessment at Gate 4:**
- Confirm all Phase 4 risks mitigated
- Identify Phase 5 (ongoing production) risks
- Plan continuous improvement initiatives

**Customer Involvement:**
- Customer receipt and approval of PPAP package
- Customer preview of control plan and quality strategy (if required)
- Customer acknowledgment of readiness for full production

## Gate 5: Feedback and Optimization Review (Program Close-Out)

**Phase:** Phase 5 (Feedback, Assessment, and Corrective Action)

**Timing:** Typically 6–12 months after SOP (or per customer timeline)

**Key Deliverables to Review:**
- Variation reduction summary and trend charts
- Customer satisfaction metrics (on-time delivery, quality PPM, complaints)
- Delivery and service performance summary
- Corrective action summary and effectiveness
- Lessons learned report
- Continuous improvement action plan

**Gate 5 Exit Criteria (decision to close program):**

1. **Production Stable**
   - No major non-conformances in past 90 days
   - Cpk/Ppk sustained at or above target (≥ 1.33 or customer minimum)
   - SPC charts show statistical control
   - Defect rate low and trending downward

2. **Variation Reduction Achieved**
   - Process optimization experiments completed (e.g., parameter studies)
   - Defect reduction data shows downward trend over time
   - Fixture or tooling improvements implemented and validated
   - Operator training effectiveness demonstrated
   - Material standardization (if applicable) completed

3. **Customer Satisfaction Metrics at Target**
   - On-time delivery ≥ 98% (or customer target)
   - Quality performance (PPM or first-pass yield) at or above target
   - Customer complaint rate low (≤ 1 per 10,000 parts, typical)
   - Customer satisfaction survey score ≥ acceptable threshold
   - No major customer escalations or audits required

4. **Delivery Performance Excellent**
   - Lead time consistency: standard deviation < 5% (or customer tolerance)
   - Expedite request frequency low
   - Inventory turns adequate (no excess stock)
   - Just-in-time (JIT) delivery capability proven (if customer requirement)
   - Logistics and supply chain seamless

5. **All Corrective Actions Closed and Effective**
   - No open corrective actions older than 30 days
   - Corrective action effectiveness verified (no repeat failures)
   - Root cause analysis (RCCA) conducted on all major issues
   - Preventive actions implemented and tracked
   - Corrective action trend: frequency declining

6. **Lessons Learned Documented and Shared**
   - Project retrospective completed with team
   - Design lessons captured (what worked, improvements for next project)
   - Manufacturing process insights documented
   - Supplier performance feedback documented
   - Organizational knowledge repository updated (if applicable)
   - Best practices identified and shared across organization

7. **Continuous Improvement Plan in Place**
   - Ongoing process optimization activities identified
   - Cost reduction initiatives (material, labor, equipment) underway or planned
   - Quality improvement targets set for sustained production
   - Kaizen events or continuous improvement cycles scheduled
   - Ownership and accountability for improvements clear

8. **Program Transition Complete**
   - Product and process transitioned from project to ongoing supply
   - Steady-state staffing and responsibilities assigned
   - Quality metrics monitoring continuing (SPC, customer feedback)
   - Supplier relationships and performance monitoring ongoing
   - Change control process in place for design/process changes

9. **Customer Sign-Off Obtained**
   - Customer acceptance of final quality and delivery metrics
   - Customer feedback on supplier performance positive
   - Customer satisfaction survey completed (if applicable)
   - Renewal or extension of business relationship confirmed

10. **Program Financially Successful**
    - Program delivered within budget (or variance explained)
    - Actual unit cost vs. target cost acceptable
    - Margin targets met or exceeded
    - Return on investment achieved or on track

**Risk Assessment at Gate 5:**
- Update risk register for sustained production risks (supply chain, customer demands)
- Plan contingencies for volume changes or design updates
- Identify any long-term improvement initiatives

**Customer Involvement:**
- Customer review of final quality and delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction feedback
- Discussion of potential design updates or volume changes for future model years

## Summary: Gate Review Decision Matrix

| Gate | Phase Exit | Key Go/No-Go Criteria | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1** | P1: Plan and Define | VOC captured, customer requirements defined, team organized, preliminary plan feasible | 4–8 weeks |
| **Gate 2** | P2: Product Design | DFMEA mitigated, DVP/DVR progressing, design reviews complete, prototype tested, drawings mature | 5–7 months |
| **Gate 3** | P3: Process Design | PFMEA mitigated, process flow documented, MSA/PPC plan approved, facility ready | 3–4 months |
| **Gate 4** | P4: Validation | PRR successful, Cpk/Ppk ≥ 1.33, PPAP ready, sustained production @ rate, customer approval | 2–4 weeks |
| **Gate 5** | P5: Feedback | Production stable, variation reduced, customer satisfaction met, lessons learned, program closed | 6–12 months |
